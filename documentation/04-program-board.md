# Community Programs Board

## Overview

The Community Programs board was designed as the operational hub for Mudandy Foundation. It centralizes the planning, execution, monitoring, and reporting of community initiatives, providing teams with a single source of truth for program management.

Instead of relying on spreadsheets or disconnected communication channels, the board enables stakeholders to collaborate in real time while maintaining visibility across the entire program lifecycle.

---

## Board Workflow

The board was structured around five operational stages:

### Program Requests

New program ideas, funding opportunities, and community requests are captured here before formal planning begins.

### Planning

Programs move into planning after approval, where objectives, funding, timelines, and resources are prepared before implementation.

### Active Programs

Approved initiatives currently being executed within communities.

### Monitoring & Evaluation

Programs that have completed implementation but remain under review for impact assessment, reporting, and performance evaluation.

### Completed Programs

Successfully completed initiatives retained for organizational reporting, auditing, and future reference.

---

## Benefits

The board enables Mudandy Foundation to:

- Standardize program management processes
- Improve visibility across all community initiatives
- Monitor program progress in real time
- Support collaboration between program teams
- Reduce manual tracking and administrative overhead
- Improve executive reporting and decision-making

This approach ensures consistent governance, improves accountability, and provides leadership with clear visibility into every stage of program delivery.
