# Business Requirements

## Business Challenge

Mudandy Foundation manages multiple community-based programs involving staff, volunteers, donors, beneficiaries, and external partners. As operations grew, existing methods of coordinating projects became increasingly difficult to manage.

The organization required a centralized platform capable of providing visibility into ongoing initiatives, improving collaboration, and reducing reliance on manual processes.

---

## Identified Challenges

The following operational challenges were identified:

- Project information was spread across multiple spreadsheets and documents.
- Team members lacked visibility into project progress.
- Volunteer assignments were difficult to monitor.
- Grant-funded activities were not centrally tracked.
- Leadership had limited access to real-time project reports.
- Manual follow-ups consumed valuable administrative time.
- Monitoring project deadlines became increasingly difficult as programs expanded.

---

## Business Requirements

The organization required a solution capable of:

- Managing multiple programs simultaneously.
- Assigning responsibilities to team members.
- Tracking project timelines and milestones.
- Monitoring project status in real time.
- Coordinating volunteers across different initiatives.
- Tracking grant-related activities.
- Supporting collaboration between departments.
- Providing executive dashboards for leadership.
- Automating repetitive administrative tasks.
- Supporting multiple project views including Kanban, Calendar and Timeline.

---

## Success Metrics

The implementation will be considered successful if it enables the organization to:

- Improve project visibility across all departments.
- Reduce manual project tracking.
- Increase accountability through ownership assignment.
- Improve collaboration among staff and volunteers.
- Deliver accurate project reports in real time.
- Standardize project management processes across programs.
